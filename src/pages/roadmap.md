# Roadmap

A look at where Morgue is headed. Plans evolve, and timing isn't promised — but this is what we're excited about and actively thinking through.

:::info[Have a request?]
The roadmap is shaped by what people actually want. If something here matters to you — or something's missing — let us know through the links on [morgueapp.com](https://morgueapp.com).
:::

## Planned

### Bring Your Own AI

Optional, opt-in AI features — with **your choice of provider**, including models you run entirely on your own machine:

- **Anthropic (Claude)**, **OpenAI**, and **Google (Gemini)** — bring your own API key.
- **Local models** via tools like **Ollama** and **LM Studio** — nothing leaves your computer.
- A simple **provider + model picker** in Settings, so you decide exactly what powers it.

What it could power: auto-tagging and descriptions for captures, smarter search, and faster organizing — all **opt-in**. Morgue stays fully usable without it, and in keeping with its local-first design, AI is never on by default and never required.

:::note[A note on local models]
Quality with **local models depends on the model you run.** A capable local vision model gives great results; a small one will be rougher. Cloud providers (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) will generally produce stronger results, while local models keep everything private and free to run. The choice — and the trade-off — is yours.
:::
