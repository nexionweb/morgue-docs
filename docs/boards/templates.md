# Board templates

Templates give a board instant structure — a labeled grid of headings, centered in your view — so you can drop assets into a layout instead of arranging from a blank canvas.

## Applying a template

1. On a board (an empty one is the natural place to start), open the **template** options.
2. Pick a template. Morgue inserts its **heading labels** laid out in a grid, centered on your current viewport.
3. Drop your assets under the headings and rearrange as needed.

## Customizing after

A template is just a starting point — everything it creates is normal board content:

- **Rename** or **resize** the heading labels.
- **Add or remove** sections.
- **Connect** items and **draw** on top as usual.

:::note[Templates add, they don't reset]
Applying a template **adds** its labels to the board; it doesn't wipe what's already there. Start from an empty board for the cleanest result.
:::
