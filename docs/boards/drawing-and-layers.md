# Free-draw & layers

Beyond item-to-item arrows, you can draw lines directly on the canvas and control the stacking order of everything on the board.

## Free-draw

1. Switch on **DRAW** mode from the toolbar.
2. **Drag** on the canvas to draw a line.
3. The line becomes a **free connector** — it isn't tied to any item, so it stays where you drew it.

Free-draw is great for circling a cluster, underlining, or sketching a relationship that isn't a clean item-to-item arrow.

## Layers (front / back)

Every item and connector has a place in the **stacking order** — what's drawn on top of what.

- Send a connector **in front of** or **behind** items with the **LINE FRONT / BACK** toggle.
- Bring items forward or send them back to get overlaps looking right.

:::tip[Lines behind, labels on top]
A common setup: keep connector lines **behind** your images so arrows don't cover artwork, and keep **text labels on top** so they're always readable.
:::
