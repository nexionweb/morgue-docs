# Connecting items

Connectors are arrows between items — for showing flow, grouping ideas, or noting "this led to that." There are two ways to make one.

## Drag-to-connect

1. **Hover** an item (or select it). A small **connect handle** (a ⊕) appears on its edge.
2. **Drag** from that handle onto another item.
3. Release — an arrow is drawn between the two.

This is the fastest way when you're working with a mouse or trackpad.

## Connect via selection

1. **Select exactly two items.**
2. Click **CONNECT** in the toolbar.

This is handy when items are far apart, and it's the keyboard-friendly path.

## Editing connectors

- **Select** a connector to adjust it.
- Move its **endpoints**, or reposition the whole line.
- Change its **color**.
- Control whether it sits in **front of or behind** items — see **[Free-draw & layers](/boards/drawing-and-layers)**.

:::note[Connectors follow their items]
An arrow anchored between two items stays attached as you move them around. Free-drawn lines (see the next page) are independent of any item.
:::
