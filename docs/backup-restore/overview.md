# Backup & restore overview

Because Morgue is **local-first**, your library lives on your machine — which means backups are in your hands, and they're simple.

## What's in a backup

A Morgue export is a single `.zip` containing **everything**:

- **Images** (the asset files)
- **Palettes** (and their colors)
- **Folders** and **tags**
- **Boards** — including their items and connectors

See exactly what's inside in **[Exporting your library](/backup-restore/exporting)**.

## When to back up

- Before a big cleanup (like running the **[duplicate finder](/organizing/duplicate-finder)**).
- Before **[moving to a new computer](/licensing-devices/moving-to-a-new-computer)** or deactivating a device.
- On a routine cadence — a monthly `.zip` to an external or cloud drive is cheap peace of mind.

## Restoring

Import a backup `.zip` to restore — even onto a brand-new install. Imports **de-duplicate automatically**, so restoring (or merging) never piles up copies. See **[Importing a backup](/backup-restore/importing)**.

## Moving the library

Separately from backups, you can relocate the whole **library folder** to another drive — see **[Moving your library](/backup-restore/moving-your-library)**.
