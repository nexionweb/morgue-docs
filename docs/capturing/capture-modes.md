# Capture modes

Morgue gives you three ways to capture a page, depending on whether you want what's on screen, the whole page, or just a slice.

![The Morgue browser extension showing the capture mode options](/img/screenshots/capturing-modes.png)

## Visible screen

Captures **exactly what's currently visible** in the browser viewport — what you can see without scrolling.

- Best for: a hero section, a specific above-the-fold layout, a single state you have on screen.

## Full page

Captures the **entire page top to bottom**, including everything below the fold.

- Best for: full landing pages, long-form layouts, capturing a whole design in one image.
- Note: very long pages produce tall images — great for reference, large in size.

## Region

Lets you **draw a box** around just the part you want, and captures that region.

- Best for: a single component, a button, a card, a piece of type — anything where you want the detail without the surrounding page.

:::tip[Which should I use?]
Reach for **Region** when you're collecting *components* and **Full page** when you're collecting *whole designs*. **Visible screen** is the quick grab for "what I'm looking at right now."
:::

## What happens after capture

However you capture, Morgue automatically pulls the page's **fonts and color palette** along with the image — see **[Auto-extracted fonts & colors](/capturing/fonts-and-colors)** — and drops it into your **All Assets** grid, ready to organize.
