# FAQ

## Licensing

**Why does my key say "inactive"?**
A new key is inactive until you activate it on a device. Enter it on the license screen and click **Activate** — see **[Activating your license](/getting-started/activating-your-license)**.

**Can I use Morgue on more than one Mac?**
Yes, up to your plan's device limit. Activate on each, and **[free a seat](/licensing-devices/managing-devices)** by deactivating a machine you no longer use.

**What happens if I'm offline?**
Morgue keeps working within a grace window and re-validates when you're back online — see **[Working offline](/licensing-devices/offline-grace)**.

## Data & privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Locally, in a library folder on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded — Morgue is **local-first**. You can **[move the folder](/backup-restore/moving-your-library)** or **[export a backup](/backup-restore/exporting)** anytime.

**Do my captures go through a server?**
No. The browser extension talks to the app over a **local connection** on your own machine.

**How do I back up?**
**Settings → Library → Export** creates a single `.zip` with everything — see **[Exporting your library](/backup-restore/exporting)**.

## Capturing

**The extension won't connect — why?**
The Morgue app must be **open** for the extension to reach it. See **[Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting)**.

**Why didn't it grab the fonts/colors I expected?**
Extraction reads what the page exposes; image-baked type or unusual rendering surfaces less — see **[Auto-extracted fonts & colors](/capturing/fonts-and-colors)**.

## Updating

**Do I need to re-download new versions?**
No — Morgue **[auto-updates](/updating/overview)**. Just click **Restart & Update** when prompted.
