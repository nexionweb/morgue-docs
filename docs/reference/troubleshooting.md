# Troubleshooting

## The extension won't connect

The extension needs the Morgue app running to receive captures.

1. **Open the Morgue app**, then reopen the extension — this fixes it most of the time.
2. **Restart the app** and try again.
3. Check the **capture bridge status** under **Settings → Extension**; restart the app if it isn't running.
4. **Restart your browser** if it still won't connect.

## Captures aren't appearing

1. Confirm the extension shows as **connected**.
2. Make sure you completed the capture (chose a **[capture mode](/capturing/capture-modes)**).
3. Check your **All Assets** view — captures land there; an active folder/tag/search filter may be hiding them. Clear filters or search.

## Activation problems

- **"Inactive" key:** normal before activation — click **Activate**.
- **Device limit reached:** Morgue shows your max; **[free a seat](/licensing-devices/managing-devices)** by deactivating an old machine, or manage devices in your **[account dashboard](https://morgueapp.com/customer-dashboard)**.
- **Locked after going offline:** reconnect to the internet so Morgue can re-validate — see **[Working offline](/licensing-devices/offline-grace)**.

## Updates won't install

- Run Morgue from **Applications**, not from inside a mounted `.dmg`.
- Check your connection and relaunch.
- Fallback: reinstall the latest `.dmg` from [morgueapp.com](https://morgueapp.com) — your library is untouched.

## Still stuck?

Take an **[export backup](/backup-restore/exporting)** (always safe), then reach out through the channels on [morgueapp.com](https://morgueapp.com).
