# Moving to a new computer

Switching Macs is a three-step hand-off: **back up**, **deactivate the old machine**, **activate the new one**.

## 1. Export your library first

On the **old** Mac, create a full backup so your assets, palettes, folders, tags, and boards come with you:

- **Settings → Library → Export** → save the `.zip` somewhere you can reach from the new machine (external drive or cloud). See **[Exporting your library](/backup-restore/exporting)**.

:::warning[Do this before deactivating]
Deactivating locks the app on the old Mac. Export **first** while you still have full access.
:::

## 2. Deactivate the old machine

- **Settings → License → Deactivate this device.** This frees the seat so you can use it on the new Mac.

## 3. Activate the new machine

1. **[Install Morgue](/getting-started/installation)** on the new Mac.
2. Enter your license key to **[activate](/getting-started/activating-your-license)**.
3. **[Import](/backup-restore/importing)** the `.zip` you exported in step 1.

You're back exactly where you left off — and because import de-duplicates, there's no harm if anything overlaps.

## If you didn't deactivate the old one

If the old Mac is gone or you forgot to deactivate, you may hit your plan's **device limit** when activating. Manage your seats from your **[account dashboard](https://morgueapp.com/customer-dashboard)**.
