# Licensing overview

Morgue unlocks with a **license key** that you activate on your devices. Here's the model at a glance.

## License keys

- Your key looks like a UUID: `xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx`.
- You enter it once on a device to **activate** — see **[Activating your license](/getting-started/activating-your-license)**.
- Find your key in your purchase email or **[account dashboard](https://morgueapp.com/customer-dashboard)**.

## Plans

Your key belongs to a **plan** (for example, *Freelancer* or *Studio*). The plan name is shown under **Settings → License** once you've activated.

## Activation limits

Each plan allows a set number of **devices** (seats). When you activate, the current device takes a seat.

- Hit the limit and Morgue tells you the maximum, and points you to manage your devices.
- Free a seat by **[deactivating](/licensing-devices/managing-devices)** a machine you no longer use.

## Working offline

After activation, Morgue keeps working **offline** within a grace window and re-validates quietly when you're back online — see **[Working offline](/licensing-devices/offline-grace)**.
