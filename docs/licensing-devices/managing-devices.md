# Managing devices

Everything about the device you're on lives under **Settings → License**.

## Viewing your device

Settings → License shows:

- Your **plan**.
- The **device name** registered for this Mac.
- Your license **status** (Active once the seat is registered).

## Renaming a device

Give this Mac a recognizable name so your device list stays readable:

1. Go to **Settings → License**.
2. Click **Rename** next to the device.
3. Enter a new name. It updates on your license.

## Freeing a seat

If you're at your plan's device limit, free a seat by **deactivating** a machine you no longer use.

- Deactivating from **Settings → License → Deactivate this device** releases that device's seat.
- The deactivated device locks and will ask for a key again next launch.

:::warning[Export before you deactivate]
Deactivating locks the app on that machine. Your **data isn't deleted**, but if you're switching machines, take an **[export backup](/backup-restore/exporting)** first so you can carry your library over. See **[Moving to a new computer](/licensing-devices/moving-to-a-new-computer)**.
:::
