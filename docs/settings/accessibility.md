# Accessibility

Morgue follows your macOS accessibility preferences and supports full keyboard navigation.

## High contrast

When you turn on **System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Increase contrast**, Morgue automatically **strengthens** borders, muted text, and focus outlines for better legibility — in both light and dark themes. There's nothing to toggle inside the app; it respects the system setting.

## Reduced motion

With **Reduce motion** enabled in macOS, Morgue **minimizes animations** (such as theme transitions) so the interface stays calm.

## Keyboard navigation

- The **asset grid** is fully keyboard-navigable: **Tab** into it, then use **arrow keys** to move, **Enter** to open or select, and **Space** to multi-select.
- A clear **focus ring** shows where you are.
- See the full list in **[Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts)**.

:::tip[Set it once, system-wide]
Because Morgue honors the OS settings, configuring contrast and motion in macOS applies them consistently across Morgue and the rest of your Mac.
:::
