# Palettes & fonts overview

Color and type are first-class citizens in Morgue, not afterthoughts on a screenshot. Every capture brings its palette and fonts along — and you can build, copy, and browse them directly.

![An asset's auto-extracted palette and detected fonts in the details panel](/img/screenshots/palette-extraction.png)

## Palettes in Morgue

- Captures arrive with an **auto-extracted palette** — see **[Auto-extracted fonts & colors](/capturing/fonts-and-colors)**.
- Build or refine palettes by hand in the **[Palette builder](/palettes-fonts/palette-builder)**.
- **[Copy any color](/palettes-fonts/copying-colors)** as hex with a click or right-click.
- Find assets by color with **[color search](/organizing/search)**.

## Fonts in Morgue

- Captures record the **fonts detected** on the page.
- Browse and preview your fonts in the **[Font browser](/palettes-fonts/font-browser)**.
- Add your **[Google Fonts API key](/palettes-fonts/google-fonts)** to browse the full Google Fonts catalog inside Morgue.

## Where they show up

Palettes and fonts appear in the **All Assets** grid alongside images (filter the grid to **Palettes** or **Fonts**), and palette colors are visible right on the cards.
