# Activating your license

The first time you open Morgue, you'll see the **license screen**. Enter your key once and the app unlocks on this device.

## Where to find your license key

Your key arrives in two places after purchase:

- Your **purchase confirmation email**.
- Your **[account dashboard](https://morgueapp.com/customer-dashboard)** at morgueapp.com.

A Morgue license key looks like a UUID — five groups of characters separated by dashes:

```
xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx
```

:::tip
Copy the key straight from the email or dashboard to avoid typos. There's no "morgue" prefix — just the key itself.
:::

## Activate

1. On the license screen, paste your key into the field.
2. Click **Activate**.
3. Morgue contacts the licensing service, registers **this device**, and unlocks. You'll land in your library.

Once activated, you'll see your details under **Settings → License**:

- **Plan** — the plan your key belongs to (for example, *Freelancer* or *Studio*).
- **Device** — the name registered for this Mac. You can **rename** it; see [Managing devices](/licensing-devices/managing-devices).
- **Status** — *Active* once the seat is registered.

## "Inactive" is normal before you activate

A brand-new key may show as **inactive** until it's activated on a device — that's expected, not an error. Entering the key and clicking **Activate** is what turns it active.

## Device limits

Each plan allows a certain number of devices (seats). If you hit the limit, Morgue tells you the maximum and points you to **manage your devices**. To free up a seat, deactivate Morgue on a machine you no longer use — see [Managing devices](/licensing-devices/managing-devices) and [Moving to a new computer](/licensing-devices/moving-to-a-new-computer).

## Working offline

After activation, Morgue **keeps working without an internet connection** within a grace window, re-validating quietly when you're back online. You won't get locked out by a flaky connection or a flight. See [Working offline](/licensing-devices/offline-grace) for exactly how the grace period behaves.
